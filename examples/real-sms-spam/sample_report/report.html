The catch. You cannot reliably find the poisoned rows, so unrelabel does the opposite: it freezes the behavior the attack targets into a canary and gates on it. An accuracy check would ship this model; the canary blocks it. Run unrelabel harden then unrelabel check to wire it into CI.
Every attack is scored on three axes: Damage (how often the targeted behavior fails), Detectability (how much overall accuracy moved), and Effort (how many training rows it took). Numbers are medians across 5 random seeds.

Findings

Model accepts a trigger-phrase backdoor critical

Planting 22 trigger examples (0.5% of training data) teaches the model that “verified secure delivery gateway ref zx9 alpha omega tango niner delta echo” means “ham”. 98% of “spam” inputs carrying the phrase were then classified “ham” (up from 31% on the clean model).

Overall accuracy changed by only 0.4 points, so a standard accuracy dashboard would likely show this model as healthy.
